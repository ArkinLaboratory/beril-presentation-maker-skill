**Throughline (TL1):** Ecotype-stratified microbiome analysis defines a
state-dependent, hybrid phage-cocktail framework for Crohn's disease
with concrete per-patient drafts.

**Deck-level register:** STRONG tier — assertive and quantitative;
claims stated as findings with their hedges attached, not buried.
Audience: computational / translational microbiome researchers. Every
content-slide title is a punchline that carries a number or a named
mechanism. Partial-evidence claims are hedged explicitly on the slide
(the ecotype framework's LOSO variance, the single-study E3 set, the
n=1 longitudinal trajectory) — honesty is part of the STRONG register,
not a separate caveats slide.

**Deck arc — framework → targets → intervention.** Each section earns
the next: ecotypes make patient stratification possible; stratification
makes confound-free target discovery possible; targets make the
cocktail framework possible. The composer for each section should make
its closing slide tee up the next section's question.

## Section S1 — Four reproducible ecotypes stratify Crohn's disease
- **Punchline:** Four reproducible microbiome ecotypes stratify Crohn's disease into biologically distinct patient groups.
- **Budget:** section divider + 4 content slides.
- **Opens the deck.** No prior section — establish the stratification premise from a standing start.
- **Carries:** A1 (four ecotypes E0–E3; K=4 consensus; 8,489 samples — hedge: LOSO ARI 0.113, a usable framework but not bit-reproducible), A2 (UC Davis CD spans three ecotypes, χ²=10.0, p=0.019), A3 (clinical covariates alone cannot assign ecotype — AUC 0.80 but only 41% patient agreement).
- **Headline-number slot:** the 8,489-sample / K=4 consensus is S1's headline (a `big_number` or `claim_evidence`).
- **Hands off to S2:** close on — "the ecotypes are biologically real but a framework, not bit-reproducible; the operational question is whether the *targets* inside each ecotype replicate." That question is S2's opening line.

## Section S2 — Confound-free analysis yields six actionable pathobiont targets
- **Punchline:** Within-ecotype, within-substudy meta-analysis surfaces six actionable pathobionts that replicate at 88% on a held-out cohort.
- **Budget:** section divider + 4 content slides.
- **Leads in from S1:** S1 closed on "do the per-ecotype targets replicate?" — open S2 by answering directly: within-ecotype × within-substudy meta-analysis strips the confounds that a naive cross-cohort scan would carry.
- **Carries:** A4 (six actionable Tier-A pathobionts, score ≥2.5, from 51 E1 meta-viable candidates), A5 (E1 Tier-A replicates at 88.2% sign concordance on held-out HMP2 — the deck's strongest credibility number), A6 (E3 Tier-A is single-study HallAB_2017 — hedge explicitly as provisional), A7 (the targets form one co-occurrence module per ecotype).
- **Headline-number slot:** 88.2% HMP2 sign-concordance — the single strongest claim in the deck; give it a `big_number`.
- **Hands off to S3:** close on — "six targets are identified and the E1 set is replicated; but can phage actually hit them?" That is S3's opening.

## Section S3 — A hybrid phage-cocktail framework with concrete per-patient drafts
- **Punchline:** A hybrid three-strategy framework turns six targets into concrete per-patient cocktail drafts for 61% of a real cohort.
- **Budget:** section divider + 5 content slides.
- **Leads in from S2:** S2 closed on "can phage hit these targets?" — open S3 with the 3-layer phage-evidence stack that answers it.
- **Carries:** A8 (a 5-phage E. coli AIEC cocktail at 95% strain coverage, from greedy set-cover over 96 phages × 188 strains), A9 (three of six targets have no lytic phage — H. hathewayi and F. plautii in the phage GAP, M. gnavus temperate-only), A10 (a pure cocktail is infeasible for the dominant E1 ecotype → the honest pivot to a hybrid 3-strategy framework), A11 (14/23 UC Davis patients — 61% — receive concrete drafts), A12 (patient 6967's E1→E3 drift motivates five state-dependent dosing rules — hedge: n=1).
- **Headline-number slot:** 95% strain coverage (A8) for the cocktail-construction slide; 61% patients-with-drafts (A11) for the deliverable slide.
- **Closes the deck:** land on the hybrid framework + per-patient drafts as the deliverable, framed honestly as a design template, not a ready-to-deploy cocktail. No hand-off — this is the final section.
